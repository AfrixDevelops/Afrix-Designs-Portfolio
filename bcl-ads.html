BC Liquor - Digital Ads

BC Liquors is a government-run retail outlets for alcohol in British Columbia. They sell a wide range of wines, spirits, beers, and coolers to the public while promoting responsible consumption.

The Objective

  • To encourage our customers to support local businesses by purchasing Canadian made products.
  • To let our customers know that BCLIQUOR offers a diverse selection of high-quality, locally crafted wine, beer, and spirits.
  • To position BCLIQUOR as the destination to go to for great sales and product offerings.
