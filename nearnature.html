Near Nature

Near Nature is a personalized outdoor activity discovery application that connects users with ideal natural spaces based on their unique preferences, leveraging advanced filtering and AI-driven recommendations.

Personalized Outdoor Exploration App for Vancouver.

  • Role: Product Designer, UX/UI Designer
  • Platform: Mobile (Web App Prototype)
  • Tools: Base44, Figma, ChatGPT
  • Timeline: 1 Week
  • Status: Concept to Functional MVP (Minimum Vaiable Product)

Goals

Design a lightweight, intuitive app that:
  • Connect users with ideal natural spaces
  • Streamline the discovery process for outdoor activities
  • Encourage engagement with nature
  • Enhance overall outdoor experiences
  • Personalize outdoor activity suggestions based on individual preferences

Pain Points

Near Nature addresses several common pain points:
  • Users often struggle to find outdoor locations that match their preferred activity (e.g., walk, hike, bike), difficulty level, or duration.
  • Scattered information across various platforms makes it hard to get a complete picture of a place's features, accessibility, and transit options.
  • Time is often wasted researching multiple locations and checking various factors before deciding on an activity.
  • Too many options without a clear way to filter and personalize can lead to decision paralysis.

Target Users

  • Local outdoor enthusiasts
  • Families with young children
  • Tourists and visitors

Design Principles

  • At its core, the app prioritizes user preferences to deliver highly relevant recommendations.
  • A clean, easy-to-navigate interface ensures a seamless user journey from discovery to planning.
  • A nature-inspired visual design, featuring soft colors and engaging imagery, creates an inviting and calming user experience.
  • Tools are designed to minimize friction, helping users find what they need quickly and confidently.

Seasonal Bloom Feed

  • Monthly bloom highlights
  • Flower-focused discovery instead of event-based listings

Location-Based Discovery

  • Curated bloom locations across Japan
  • Easy scanning by region and flower type

Bloom Alerts (Concept)

  • Notify users when blooms reach peak season
  • Designed as opt-in and non-intrusive

Map View

  • Visual exploration of bloom spots
  • Ideal for trip planning and spontaneous exploration

Challenges

  • Balancing simplicity with usefulness
  • Designing for users unfamiliar with Japanese geography
  • Creating value without personalization or accounts
  • Working within Base44’s constraints while maintaining polish
  • Manually adding the images in the database because AI cant source it
