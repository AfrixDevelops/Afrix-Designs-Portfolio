Find Hanami

Find Hanami is a mobile-first app designed to help tourists and locals in Japan discover seasonal flower blooms—what’s blooming now, where to find it, and when to go. Japan’s natural beauty is deeply seasonal, but bloom information is often fragmented across local tourism sites, blogs, and social media. Find Hanami centralizes this information into a simple, location-based experience that makes seasonal exploration effortless.

Seasonal Flower Discovery App for Japan

  • Role: Product Designer, UX/UI Designer
  • Platform: Mobile (Web App Prototype)
  • Tools: Base44, Figma, ChatGPT
  • Timeline: 3 Weeks
  • Status: Concept to Functional MVP (Minimum Vaiable Product)

Goals

Design a lightweight, intuitive app that:
  • Shows current and upcoming blooms
  • Highlights where to see them
  • Works quickly on-the-go
  • Requires no account or onboarding friction

Pain Points

Travelers in Japan often struggle to:
  • Know which flowers are currently blooming
  • Find reliable, location-specific bloom spots
  • Plan visits around short bloom windows
  • Avoid information overload from scattered sources

Target Users

  • Tourists visiting Japan
  • First-time visitors unfamiliar with regional bloom cycles
  • Casual travelers who want quick, visual answers
  • Photography and nature enthusiasts

Design Principles

  • Season-first: Time and nature drive the experience
  • Low friction: No login, no setup, no learning curve
  • Clarity over depth: Useful info, not encyclopedic content
  • Mobile priority: Designed for outdoor, one-handed use

Seasonal Bloom Feed

  • Monthly bloom highlights
  • Flower-focused discovery instead of event-based listings

Location-Based Discovery

  • Curated bloom locations across Japan
  • Easy scanning by region and flower type

Bloom Alerts (Concept)

  • Notify users when blooms reach peak season
  • Designed as opt-in and non-intrusive

Map View

  • Visual exploration of bloom spots
  • Ideal for trip planning and spontaneous exploration

Challenges

  • Balancing simplicity with usefulness
  • Designing for users unfamiliar with Japanese geography
  • Creating value without personalization or accounts
  • Working within Base44’s constraints while maintaining polish
  • Manually adding the images in the database because AI cant source it

Outcome

Find Hanami demonstrates my ability to:
  • Identify a focused travel problem
  • Design a clear MVP with restraint
  • Make intentional product decisions
  • Build and ship a functional app, not just screens
